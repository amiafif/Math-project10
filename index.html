The Bioscience Problem

                

                

                    The indigenous **Kedah-Kelantan (KK) cattle** form the backbone of beef production in Malaysia due to their extreme climate tolerance and parasite resistance. However, traditional KK cattle have historically suffered from low growth rates and small mature sizes. 
                

                

                    To optimize profit margins, farm managers must know the exact age at which a herd's growth rate peaks. In animal science, the instantaneous growth rate—modeled mathematically as the **first derivative \(f'(x)\)**—corresponds to the animal's **Average Daily Gain (ADG)**. Identifying this peak inflection point helps nutritional technologists time the transition between high-protein weaning supplements (e.g., Palm Kernel Cake pellets) and maintenance roughage.
                

            


            
            

                

                    
                    
Calculus in Action: Gompertz Model

                

                

                    The growth curve is modeled using a standard biological sigmoidal (Gompertz) function:
                

                
                
                

                    

                        Weight Curve Function:
                        \[f(x) = A \cdot e^{-B \cdot e^{-k \cdot x}}\]
                    

                    

                    

                        Growth Rate (Derivative):
                        \[f'(x) = A \cdot B \cdot k \cdot e^{-k \cdot x} \cdot e^{-B \cdot e^{-k \cdot x}}\]
                        
Using Chain Rule, since \(f(x) = A e^u\) where \(u = -B e^{-k x}\)

                    

                


                

                    

                        \(A\) (Asymptote)
                        Target mature weight (kg) at full size.
                    

                    

                        \(B\) (Constant)
                        Integration scale linked to birth weight.
                    

                    

                        \(k\) (Maturing)
                        Rate of growth towards maturity.
